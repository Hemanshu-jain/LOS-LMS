1. Who this policy covers and our role

LOS/LMS is software that regulated lenders (banks, NBFCs and other finance companies, each a “Lender”) use to originate, verify, sanction, disburse and collect loans. Bhodhix does not lend money and is not a party to any loan.

  • Lender staff and administrators. For the people who sign in to the Platform, Bhodhix is the “Data Fiduciary” under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”).
  • Borrowers, co-applicants, guarantors and other individuals whose details a Lender enters or collects. The Lender decides why and how that data is used and is the Data Fiduciary. Bhodhix acts as the Lender’s “Data Processor” and handles that data only on the Lender’s instructions, under a contract with the Lender.

If you are a borrower, the Lender’s own privacy notice also applies to you and is your first point of contact for any request about your loan data.

3. Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To create and secure accounts, authenticate users and keep audit trails.
  • To let the Lender run its loan process: identity and KYC verification, credit assessment inputs, sanction, agreement generation and e-Sign, e-NACH mandate registration, disbursement records, servicing and collections.
  • To bill our customers for subscriptions and for third-party verification usage.
  • To comply with law, respond to lawful requests from courts and authorities, and prevent fraud and misuse.
  • To maintain, secure, back up and improve the Platform.

The legal basis is the consent of the individual, given to the Lender and, where we collect directly, to us; or, where the DPDP Act permits, a legitimate use such as compliance with law or performance of a function under law. Where we rely on consent you may withdraw it at any time (section 9). Withdrawal does not affect processing already done, and it may prevent a loan or a mandate from continuing.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use borrower data for our own marketing or to build advertising profiles.

4. Aadhaar, PAN and other sensitive identifiers

  • Aadhaar-based verification and Aadhaar e-Sign are offered only with the individual’s explicit consent and only through Digio, an entity authorised to provide these services. The individual completes the OTP step on the provider’s own screens.
  • We do not collect or store fingerprints, iris scans or other biometric authentication data. Aadhaar data is used only for the purpose for which consent was given (KYC or e-Sign) and is handled in line with the Aadhaar Act, 2016 and the related regulations and UIDAI circulars.
  • PAN, bank and UPI details are used to verify identity and to set up repayments, and are visible only to users of the relevant Lender who need them for their role.

5. Who we share data with

  • The Lender that the data belongs to. Each Lender sees only its own data; the Platform separates every company’s records.
  • Verification and signing provider: Digio for KYC, DigiLocker, PAN, bank and UPI verification, e-Sign and e-NACH, and through it the relevant networks such as UIDAI, NPCI and banks.
  • Infrastructure providers (processors): Render (application hosting), Aiven (managed database), Supabase (document and image storage), Cloudflare (DNS and network security), GitHub (source code and encrypted backups) and an email delivery provider for system emails. Each is bound by its own terms to protect the data.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Partner software that a Lender has explicitly linked to its company account (for example a collections or accounting application) through the Platform’s Connect feature. The Lender can disconnect it at any time.
  • Authorities and professional advisers where we are legally required to, to enforce our rights, or to obtain legal advice, in each case sharing the minimum necessary.

6. Where data is stored and transferred

Some of our infrastructure providers operate data centres outside India (our application is hosted in Singapore). Personal data may therefore be transferred outside India. We do so only to the extent permitted by section 16 of the DPDP Act and any restriction notified by the Government of India, and we require equivalent safeguards from providers. Payment-mandate data is processed by Digio and by the banking network in India. If a Lender’s regulator requires data to stay in India, the Lender should tell us so that we can discuss the available options.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, and for as long as law requires. Lenders are generally required to keep KYC and transaction records for at least five years after the end of the customer relationship under the Prevention of Money-laundering Act, 2002 and RBI directions, and other records for the periods set by law. When retention is no longer needed we erase or irreversibly anonymise the data. Encrypted backups roll off on a fixed schedule, so deleted data may remain in backups for a limited period before they expire.

8. How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted nightly backups.
  • Passwords stored only in hashed form; optional Google sign-in; forced password change for temporary passwords.
  • Role-based access and strict separation between Lender accounts; audit logs of sensitive actions.
  • Partner connections use short-lived tokens that are stored only as one-way hashes on our side and can be revoked by the Lender.
  • Secrets are kept in protected configuration, not in source code, and access is limited to people who need it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and the affected parties (through the Lender where we are the processor) in the manner and time the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may: (a) ask for a summary of the personal data processed and of the parties it was shared with; (b) ask for correction, completion or updating of your data; (c) ask for erasure, subject to legal retention duties; (d) withdraw consent; (e) seek grievance redressal; and (f) nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.

How to ask. Borrowers should write to their Lender first. If you write to us, we will forward the request to the Lender or act on it as the Lender instructs, and we aim to reply within 30 days. For data we hold as a Data Fiduciary (Platform users), write to us directly at the address below. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

If you are not satisfied with our response you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is constituted and operational.

10. Children

The Platform is for lenders and for adult borrowers. We do not knowingly process the personal data of anyone under 18 without the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian as the DPDP Act requires, and we do not track or target children.

11. Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies: a sign-in session cookie and anti-forgery cookies that protect forms. We also store small display preferences (such as column widths) in your browser. You can clear these in your browser settings, but you will then be signed out.
