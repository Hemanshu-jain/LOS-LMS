1. About this document

These terms explain how electronic repayment mandates (e-NACH, also called auto-debit mandates) work when a Lender uses LOS/LMS to register one for a borrower. They supplement, and do not replace, the loan agreement and the mandate form that the borrower authorises. In a conflict about the loan or its charges, the loan agreement prevails. Bhodhix is a technology provider only: the lender is the party that collects the repayments, and Bhodhix does not hold, receive or move the borrower’s money.

2. How a mandate is set up

  1. The Lender enters or confirms the borrower’s bank account, the maximum amount, the frequency and the start and end dates.
  2. The borrower receives a link, opens the secure mandate screen provided by our provider Digio, picks their bank, and authenticates through net banking, debit card or Aadhaar e-Sign, as offered by the bank.
  3. The bank confirms the authorisation, and the mandate is registered through the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) NACH system via the lender’s sponsor bank.
  4. The Platform shows the mandate status to the Lender (for example pending, active, rejected or cancelled).

Authentication happens on the bank’s or the provider’s screens. We never see or store the borrower’s net-banking credentials, card PIN, OTP or CVV.

3. What the borrower authorises

By completing the mandate the borrower authorises the Lender, through its sponsor bank, to instruct the borrower’s bank to debit the account on or around each due date for the instalments and other amounts payable under the loan agreement, up to the maximum amount per debit shown on the mandate, for the period of the mandate or until the loan is fully repaid, whichever is earlier. The borrower confirms that they are the holder or an authorised signatory of the account, that the details are correct, and that they will keep enough balance in the account on each due date.

4. Charges

Any fee for registering or presenting a mandate, and any charge for a failed (bounced) debit, is set by the Lender in the loan agreement and by the borrower’s and the sponsor banks under their tariffs. Bhodhix does not charge the borrower. Penal or bounce charges, if any, are determined and collected by the Lender and must be disclosed in the loan agreement and key fact statement.

5. Failed debits

A debit can fail for reasons such as insufficient balance, a closed or frozen account, an exceeded limit, an account-number mismatch or a bank-side error. A failed debit does not cancel the amount owed. The Lender may present it again or ask the borrower to pay by another method, and the loan agreement governs late-payment consequences.

6. Changing or cancelling a mandate

  • The borrower can ask to cancel or change a mandate by writing to the Lender, or through their own bank’s mandate-management option where the bank offers it. A cancellation request takes effect once the bank and the sponsor bank process it, which may take a few working days, so a debit already presented may still go through.
  • Cancelling a mandate does not cancel the loan. The borrower must continue to pay on time by another method agreed with the Lender.
  • The Lender may cancel a mandate when the loan is repaid or closed, or when a replacement mandate is registered.

7. Wrong, duplicate or unauthorised debits

  1. Contact the Lender at once, with the date, amount and bank reference of the debit. You should also tell your bank.
  2. The Lender is responsible for investigating and, where the debit was wrongful or in excess of what was due, for returning the money promptly, in line with its loan agreement, RBI regulations and NPCI NACH rules.
  3. If the Lender does not resolve the matter you may complain to your bank, then to the Reserve Bank of India Integrated Ombudsman Scheme at cms.rbi.org.in, and may use any other remedy the law gives you.

Bhodhix can supply the Lender with the mandate and debit records it holds to help resolve a dispute.

8. Data used for the mandate

To register and run the mandate, the borrower’s name, mobile number, email, bank name, account number and IFSC, and the amount and schedule are shared with Digio, the Lender’s sponsor bank, NPCI and the borrower’s bank. This is described further in our Privacy Policy.

9. Governing rules and contact

Mandates are subject to the NPCI NACH procedural guidelines and the applicable RBI directions, as amended from time to time. Questions about a mandate on your loan should go to your Lender first. For a question about this document or the Platform, write to contact@bhodhix.com.
