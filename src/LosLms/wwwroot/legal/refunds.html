1. Scope

This policy covers money that a Customer (a lender or other business using LOS/LMS) pays to Bhodhix: subscription fees, usage charges for third-party verification services, and any other invoice we raise. It does not cover loan amounts, EMIs, processing fees, penal charges or other money that a borrower pays to a lender; those are governed by the borrower’s loan agreement and the lender’s own policy, because Bhodhix does not collect, hold or refund borrower money (see section 6).

2. Subscription fees and cancellation

  • A Customer may cancel its subscription at any time by writing to contact@bhodhix.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid billing period; access continues until then and no further subscription charge is raised.
  • Subscription fees for a period that has begun are not refunded pro rata for unused time, unless a written agreement says otherwise.
  • If the Platform was not made available to you for a period because of our fault (not caused by third-party providers, your own systems or scheduled maintenance), we will, on request, credit or refund the subscription fee for that period in proportion to the unavailability.

3. Usage charges for verification, e-Sign and e-NACH

Each billable request (such as a PAN, bank-account, UPI, vehicle or business check, an e-Sign document, or an e-NACH mandate) is a cost that Bhodhix incurs with its provider at the moment the request runs. These charges are therefore non-refundable once the request has been executed, including when the result is a mismatch or a rejection, with these exceptions that we will credit or refund:

  • A duplicate charge for one request caused by an error in the Platform.
  • A charge for a request that failed because of a fault on our side or our provider’s side, and for which our provider did not charge us.
  • A charge billed at the wrong rate against the Customer’s agreed rate card.
  • Prepaid or paid balances that were credited in error.

4. How to ask for a refund or correct an invoice

  1. Email contact@bhodhix.com within 30 days of the invoice or payment date with the invoice number, the amount, and the transaction (for example the application number, request type and date).
  2. We acknowledge within 2 business days and aim to decide within 7 business days. We may ask for supporting details, and we check the request against our usage and billing records.
  3. If approved, the refund is made to the original payment source (bank account or UPI ID the payment came from) within 7 to 10 business days, or, if you prefer, applied as a credit against future invoices. For payments made through bank transfer or UPI we match the UTR reference you gave us. A GST credit note is issued for an invoiced amount that is refunded.
  4. If we decline, we will give the reason in writing.

5. Payments made in error

If you pay the wrong amount, pay twice, or pay an invoice that was cancelled, tell us with the UTR or transaction reference and we will refund or credit the excess in line with section 4. Bank or payment-network charges that we cannot recover are not refunded.

6. Borrower payments, loan charges and e-NACH debits

  • Bhodhix is a software provider, not a lender, and is not the beneficiary of a borrower’s EMI or other loan payments. Questions about a loan charge, processing fee, penal charge, foreclosure amount, or a refund of any of these must go to the lender.
  • For an e-NACH debit that a borrower believes was wrong, duplicated or unauthorised, see the e-NACH Mandate Terms, section 7: the borrower should contact the lender first, and the lender is responsible for correcting and refunding the debit.

7. Contact and escalation

Write to contact@bhodhix.com. If you are unhappy with the response, escalate to our Grievance Officer at contact@bhodhix.com, who will reply within 15 business days. Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under applicable consumer or contract law.
